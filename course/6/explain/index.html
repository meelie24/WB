06

Understand the results and changes

Read the evidence behind the result and keep recalculations and versions separate.

Learning stage 1 of 4
1Explain2Show me3Do it with me4Try it myself

Words you’ll need

Sent / completed. Invitations issued versus responses actually submitted; they are different counts.

01

Check which results you are looking at

Select the survey and inspect Version, Created On, Sent Count and Completed Count before analysis. View Surveys opens individual entities and answers. Pending invitations are not completed responses. A version and its response population provide context for the numbers.

02

Use all four analysis actions

View Surveys opens individual answers. Download exports responses to Excel in the real tool; this mock produces clearly labelled Excel-friendly CSV. Calculate risk score refreshes analysis after changed weights. Aggregate Analysis displays the overall risk distribution. The guide also describes review/deletion actions; the training companion performs no real deletion.

03

Read charts as evidence, not a verdict

The guide describes comparing the lowest/highest submitted values and an entity’s relative position. In its example chart, bars show the response distribution and a blue line marks the selected respondent. Fragmentation 5 gives broader intervals; 50 or 100 gives finer display detail. It does not change weights or underlying risk. One response is not enough for a meaningful comparative population.

04

Look below the overall result

A low overall band, or no entity in the highest band, can hide elevated exposure in an individual question or category. Examine individual answers and use Excel for targeted deeper analysis, including items without particular platform weights. A chart or overall colour does not replace supervisory judgment.

05

Separate three kinds of change

After sending, do not add categories or reassign question categories. You may change weights and then recalculate. Editing even one question creates a new version with separate aggregate responses. Sending the unchanged form to more entities keeps the same version; another invitation alone is not a new version.
